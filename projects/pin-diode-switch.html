PIN-diode RF switch

A high-power RF switch for 10 MHz – 2.5 GHz, explored in three topologies: a PIN-diode DPDT, a TEE variant and a discrete-transistor SPDT.

Band
10 MHz – 2.5 GHz
Power
up to 50 W peak
Isolation target
≥ 50 dB
Tools
KiCad · [simulator]

Requirements

  • Isolation ≥ 50 dB across the band with minimum insertion loss.
  • Cold switching — RF is off during transitions; only one input is ever live.
  • Two inputs, two outputs (DPDT).

Three topologies

  • Series-shunt PIN DPDT — MPN7453B in the series position; shunt diodes chosen for Ct ≤ 0.3 pF, Rs ≤ 0.3 Ω, Vbr ≥ 250 V.
  • TEE variant — MA4P505 PIN diodes biased by a DRV8844A driver (0 / 48 V), 10 W target.
  • Discrete-transistor SPDT — 20 W CW, > 45 dB isolation, rails within ±50 V, transistors around €5 each.
